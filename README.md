# EduGuide AI – Personalized Learning Assistant

## Project Overview

EduGuide AI is an AI-powered personalized learning assistant designed to support students with learning, study planning, career guidance, and skill development.

The project addresses Sustainable Development Goal 4 (SDG 4) – Quality Education by providing students with accessible and personalized educational guidance through an AI chatbot.

## SDG Alignment

### SDG 4 – Quality Education

EduGuide AI supports SDG 4 by helping learners access educational guidance, learning resources, study strategies, career preparation, and personalized learning roadmaps.

## Problem Statement

Students often struggle to find suitable learning resources, organize their study time, identify required skills, and understand career preparation pathways.

EduGuide AI provides a single conversational platform where students can ask education and career-related questions and receive relevant guidance.

## Project Objective

The main objective of EduGuide AI is to provide students with an interactive AI learning assistant that can:

- Provide study guidance
- Suggest learning resources
- Create learning roadmaps
- Provide career guidance
- Recommend skills for different careers
- Provide interview preparation tips
- Help students manage study time
- Support personalized learning goals

## Key Features

- AI-powered chatbot
- Study preparation guidance
- Study time management tips
- Personalized learning roadmap
- AI/ML learning roadmap
- Learning resource recommendations
- Career guidance
- Data Analyst skill recommendations
- Interview preparation
- Learning goal assistance
- Fallback response for unrelated questions
